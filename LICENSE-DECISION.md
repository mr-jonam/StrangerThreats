## Why this license

The intended permission is to let people study, fork, adapt, and improve the
original material while preserving credit, the source link, change notices, and
the same non-commercial sharing terms. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 expresses those terms for
documentation more reliably than a custom licence text.

It does **not** prevent copying, guarantee that all misuse can be detected, or
replace legal advice. A public GitHub repository is visible and may be forked
under GitHub's platform terms; this licence sets the copyright permissions for
the original material. Enforcement depends on the facts, jurisdiction, evidence,
and professional legal advice.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Result | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CC BY-SA 4.0 | Not selected | Allows commercial reuse, contrary to the stated goal. |
| MIT or Apache-2.0 | Not selected | Designed for software and permits commercial reuse. |
| Custom restrictive terms | Not selected | Less interoperable and requires jurisdiction-specific legal review. |
| Private repository plus agreement | Consider when access must be controlled | A public repository cannot revoke platform visibility or existing forks. |
