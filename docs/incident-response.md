# Incident response

Incident response protects people, operations, evidence, and trust while restoring
normal service. Preparation determines how many safe options remain under pressure.

## Lifecycle

### Prepare

Define severity, authority, roles, out-of-band communications, evidence handling,
legal and regulatory contacts, suppliers, recovery objectives, and decision logs.
Exercise the plan with realistic scenarios.

### Detect and analyze

Validate the signal, establish a timeline, identify affected assets and identities,
scope uncertainty, preserve evidence, assess impact, and set an update cadence.

### Contain, eradicate, recover

Choose containment based on harm, operational cost, evidence risk, and adversary
awareness. Remove persistence and root causes, rotate affected trust material,
restore from verified sources, monitor for recurrence, and obtain business sign-off.

### Learn

Run a blame-aware review. Track corrective actions to owners and deadlines. Update
detections, architecture, training, supplier controls, and response plans.

## First-hour checklist

- Open an incident record and name an incident lead.
- Confirm scope, severity, safety constraints, and communication channel.
- Start an immutable decision and evidence log with timezone.
- Preserve volatile evidence when valuable and feasible.
- Identify business owner, legal/privacy needs, and critical dependencies.
- Define the next decision point and update time.

Use the [incident report template](../templates/incident-report.md) to keep facts,
actions, and unresolved questions aligned.
