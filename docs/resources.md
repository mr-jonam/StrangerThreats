## Source evaluation checklist

- Is it primary, reproducible, current, and within scope?
- What observation window, geography, telemetry, and sample are represented?
- Does the publisher have a commercial, policy, or attribution incentive?
- Can the material claim be corroborated independently?
- What decision will this source change?
